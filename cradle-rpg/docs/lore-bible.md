## 1. Sacred Valley Overview

### 1.1 Geography

Sacred Valley is an isolated, ringed valley in the far west of the Blackflame Empire's continent, home to
roughly a million people. It is walled in by four sacred mountains, each held by one of the four Schools.
Beneath the valley lies the **Labyrinth** (later-book canon), the true cause of the valley's stunted sacred arts.

| Landmark | Position | Held by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mount Samara** | East | Heaven's Glory School | Crowned by **Samara's Ring**, a halo of white light that appears at sundown and vanishes at sunrise; built by an expert centuries ago so the school can harvest light aura even on moonless nights. Visible from the whole valley. |
| **The Greatfather** | South | Holy Wind School | Largest peak; the Li clan's territory lies near it. |
| **Mount Venture** | West | Golden Sword School | Mined for ore; the Kazan clan's mountain territory nearby is rich in earth aura and yields **halfsilver** and **goldsteel**. |
| **Yoma Mountain** | North (unverified direction) | Fallen Leaf School | Orchard-covered; the school processes spirit-fruits into goods and secret elixirs. |
| **Wei clan territory** | Northeast valley floor, in sight of Samara's Ring | Wei clan | Purple-roofed town; dominated by **Elder Whisper's white tower**, a needle-thin landmark dating to the founders' age. |
| **The ancestral orus tree** | Wilderness in/near Wei territory | (wild) | Ancient spirit-tree; one bite of its fruit delivers years' worth of purified vital aura. Plot-critical (see §5, Beat 2). |

> **Correction to game proposal:** The Wei clan does **not** hold a "Yoki mountain." The northern peak is
> **Yoma Mountain** (Fallen Leaf School). The Wei clan lives on the valley floor northeast, beneath
> Mount Samara's light.

### 1.2 The Suppression Field (game-relevant framing)

- In *Unsouled* itself, nobody knows it exists. The valley's people simply believe **Jade is the practical
  peak of advancement** and treat Gold as semi-mythical — a stage of legend or heresy.
- (later-book canon) Sacred Valley sits over the Labyrinth and **suppresses anyone above Jade toward
  Jade-level power**, and stunts everyone's growth. This is why the **Sword Sage — a Sage, vastly beyond
  Gold — could be murdered by Jades**, and why Yerin fights below her real strength while inside.
- **Game use:** justify the level cap inside the valley; Yerin reads as "strong Jade+" in-valley; leaving the
  valley is the natural end-of-act power unlock. Li Markuth appearing at full power is a heavens-level
  anomaly, not something the field handles (unverified mechanism — treat as "he broke the rules").

### 1.3 The Three Clans

Verified: the valley's population is split into exactly three clans — **Wei, Li, Kazan**.

| Clan | Territory | Path / aspects | Flavor & politics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wei** | Northeast, under Mount Samara | **Path of the White Fox** — light + dream madra; illusion specialists | Proud, image-obsessed, ritualized courtesy ("this one is honored…"). Ruling family is the **Jin** family (Patriarch Wei Jin Sairus). Aligned-with/overshadowed by Heaven's Glory. Treats its Unsouled as a stain on clan honor. |
| **Li** | South, near the Greatfather | Not detailed in book 1. **INVENTED FOR GAME:** *Path of the Sunset Lake* — water + light madra; flowing deflection (Enforcer) and dazzling spray (Striker). | Described as **ostentatious**. Their Grand Patriarch of eight centuries past, **Li Markuth**, ascended to the heavens — and returns as the book's inciting cataclysm. Bitter ancestral rivalry with the Wei. |
| **Kazan** | West, near Mount Venture | Not detailed in book 1. **INVENTED FOR GAME:** *Path of the Mountain's Spine* — earth madra; stone-skin Enforcer focus and Forged stone weapons. | Miners of **halfsilver and goldsteel** from earth-aura-rich slopes. Blunt, physical, clan-of-laborers reputation. Sell the valley's rarest materials, which gives them quiet leverage. |

### 1.4 The Four Schools

Discipleship at the mountain schools is granted only to the most gifted (or, as Lindon proves, the most
persistent liar). Note: the game proposal listed three schools; canon has **four**.

| School | Mountain | Path / aspects | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heaven's Glory School** | Mount Samara | **Path of Heaven's Glory** — light madra (heat/radiance), gathered from Samara's Ring | Book 1's institutional villain: murders the Sword Sage for his treasures, holds Yerin captive in all but name. Self-righteous, "mandate of heaven" rhetoric. |
| **Holy Wind School** | The Greatfather | Wind + light + dream madra; creates zones of unnatural weather, storm attacks | Strange, insular weather-shamans. |
| **Golden Sword School** | Mount Venture | Sword madra; Enforcer techniques on their **goldsteel** swords (the name comes from the metal, not gold madra) | Duelist culture. |
| **Fallen Leaf School** | Yoma Mountain | Not detailed in book 1; alchemists/processors of spirit-fruit orchards. **INVENTED FOR GAME:** *Path of the Falling Leaf* — life + sweetness/decay aspects; healing salves, debuff pollen clouds (Ruler). | The valley's elixir economy runs through them. |

### 1.5 Inter-faction politics (book-1 snapshot)

- The **Seven-Year Festival** rotates hosting among the factions; it is equal parts tournament, market,
  marriage-mart, and cold-war summit. Gift exchanges between factions are status moves with teeth.
- **Heaven's Glory ↔ Wei:** patron and client; Heaven's Glory takes offense when an Unsouled (Lindon)
  wins festival matches, and the Wei Patriarch sacrifices Lindon's dignity to appease them.
- **Wei ↔ Li:** oldest and hottest rivalry (Li Markuth's grudge is aimed at the whole valley but lands at a
  Wei-hosted festival).
- **Kazan:** material suppliers, resentful third wheel (unverified nuance).
- **Game use:** clan choice at character creation should change festival match-ups, gift-exchange scenes,
  and which elders sneer at you.

## 2. The Sacred Arts System

### 2.1 Core concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Madra** | The energy of the spirit; every living thing generates it. It takes on **aspects** (light, dream, sword, wind, earth…) from the vital aura a sacred artist cycles. Pure, aspect-less madra (Lindon's) is considered worthless in the valley — outside, it is vanishingly rare and precious. |
| **Core** | The reservoir in the spirit where madra pools. Lindon's core is **weak and undeveloped, not absent** — "Unsouled" means his spirit showed *no affinity* at testing, and is feeble, not literally empty. |
| **Cycling** | Breathing/meditation patterns that move madra through the body's channels to strengthen the spirit and recover spent madra. Each Path has its own cycling technique. **Game mechanic hook:** cycling = out-of-combat regen stance / in-combat channel that trades vulnerability for resource recovery. |
| **Vital aura** | Ambient power of the natural world: light aura around Samara's Ring, fire aura in flames, sword aura on every blade's edge, earth aura in the Kazan mines, life aura in forests, dream aura around minds. Invisible below Copper. |
| **Ruler vs. aura** | Sacred artists cycle aura into themselves to advance, and Ruler techniques command it directly in the environment. |

### 2.2 Advancement stages (Foundation → Gold)

Badges (a Sacred Valley custom, not universal Cradle canon): each stage is marked by a badge of the
matching material, engraved with the bearer's primary technique type — **shield = Enforcer, arrow =
Striker, scepter = Ruler, hammer = Forger** (unverified symbol set). Lindon's badge is **wood, engraved
with the character for "empty"** (unverified detail) — a public mark of shame.

| Stage | Requirement | What you gain | Game notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Childhood stage: build the core, learn to cycle | Basic madra manipulation; children's techniques | Lindon is stuck here at 15, years past the normal age. Player "Unsouled" start = Foundation with pure madra. |
| **Copper** | Open the spiritual senses to **perceive vital aura** | **Copper sight** — seeing the world overlaid with colored aura; can now cycle aura to grow madra aspects | First "the world is bigger than I knew" moment; good tutorial gate for aura-based puzzles. |
| **Iron** | Use vital aura to **forge the body** — an ordeal of intense pain, traditionally undergone with clan elixirs and supervision | Superhuman strength, speed, durability; no more mundane sickness | The valley's clans teach **flawed Iron bodies** (the Wei don't even know perfect ones exist — later-book canon). Forging ritual = strong set-piece: a pain-endurance minigame with permanent stat consequences. |
| **Jade** | Purify and refine the spirit (the parasite ring is traditionally a Jade-stage training tool) | **Spiritual sense** — actively scan spirits, madra, scripts; read another artist's Path and power | Valley elders cap out here. Detection/stealth mechanics unlock. |
| **Gold** | **Absorb a Remnant into your core** (classically, the Remnant of a master on your own Path) | Massive power jump; a **Goldsign** — a permanent physical mark of the Remnant (Yerin's: a blood-red metallic sword-arm over her shoulder) | In-valley belief: Gold is mythical/heretical. Yerin reaches Gold *on-page* by absorbing her master's Remnant. End-of-game power fantasy. |

### 2.3 Currency, materials, elixirs

| Item | Canon facts | Game use |
|---|---|---|
| **Scales** | Currency **Forged from madra** — flat coins/scales of solidified madra; value varies by aspect and density. Standard money in the wider world; used in the valley at the Festival (unverified how universally). | Single currency; clan-aspected cosmetic variants. |
| **Halfsilver** | Metal that **disrupts/erodes madra on contact**. Mined by the Kazan. Used in dueling shackles and the parasite ring's braid. | Anti-mage weapon coating; "silence" debuff material. |
| **Goldsteel** | Mined near Mount Venture; the Golden Sword School's blades. Harmful/anathema to spirits and Remnants (unverified exact property). | Anti-Remnant damage type. |
| **Parasite ring** | A ring of **braided halfsilver etched with script**; worn, it **slows and strains cycling — "weight training for the soul,"** making training harder but far more rewarding. One sits in Heaven's Glory's Lesser Treasure Hall; **Lindon steals it during the escape** (§5 Beat 14). (Its famous daily-use regimen is book-2 material under Eithan.) | Equippable trainer item: −madra regen in combat, +XP/advancement rate. |
| **Orus fruit (ancestral)** | Fruit of an ancient orus tree; one bite grants **years' worth of purified vital aura** to process into madra. Ordinary orus fruit is just food. | Rare permanent stat consumable; opening-act MacGuffin. |
| **Starlotus bud** | Spirit-elixir that nourishes a weak spirit/core (unverified; Heaven's Glory dispenses elixirs of this kind to disciples) | Madra-max upgrade consumable. |
| **Sylvan riverseed** | A tiny living spirit of pure water-aspected... actually of **purified madra**; stolen from the Lesser Treasure Hall with the cloud and ring. In later books it becomes Little Blue. | Pet/companion item; restores damaged spirits (its later-book power) — keep as a mysterious treasure in book-1 scope. |
| **Thousand-Mile Cloud** | A Forged madra construct: a floating cloud platform ridden like a sled/mount. Stolen in the same theft; it is the escape vehicle off Mount Samara. | Mount + escape-sequence vehicle. |
| **Dream tablets** | Script-bound stones that store recorded memories/experiences viewable by touch (unverified presence in book 1; common Cradle tech). | Lore collectibles / tutorial delivery. |
| **Scripts** | Runic circles that channel/constrain madra — on rings, arenas, boundaries, wards. Lindon, lacking power, is unusually good at *using* scripts and tools. | Crafting/trap system for low-power builds. |

## 3. Technique Grammar (the four technique types)

Canon taxonomy — every technique on every Path is one of these (plus pure-madra oddities):

| Type | Canon definition | Combat-design read |
|---|---|---|
| **Enforcer** | Cycle madra through your **own body or weapon** to reinforce it | Buffs, stances, weapon glows |
| **Striker** | Project madra **outward as an attack** | Projectiles, beams, breath weapons |
| **Ruler** | Command **vital aura in the environment** to act for you | AoE/zone control; needs matching aura present (sword aura needs blades, light aura needs light) |
| **Forger** | Solidify madra into **lasting physical form** | Summons, constructs, walls, illusion-objects, currency |

In Sacred Valley, children take the **Spiritual Origin test** (hand into reactive madra/water) to reveal
their affinity for one of the four; the result is engraved on their badge. Lindon's test showed **nothing** —
hence "Unsouled."

### 3.1 Path of the White Fox (Wei clan) — light + dream madra (illusion)

Created by and named for **Elder Whisper**, the white sacred fox who co-founded the clan.

| Technique | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Fox Dream** | Ruler | Tricks the target's perception — illusory threats and full sensory experiences woven from dream aura. |
| **Fox Fire** | Striker | Purple illusory flames that look, sound, and *feel* real but do no physical damage (panic/morale weapon). |
| **Invisible Flame** | Striker | A flame attached to a target that only the caster can see (marking/tracking). (unverified book-1 appearance) |
| **Phantom Lights** | Striker | False lights bloom in the enemy's vision, blinding/confusing. (unverified book-1 appearance) |
| **Fox Tail** | Enforcer | Desyncs your visible image from your true position — your movements lie. |
| **White Fox Forgery** | Forger | Lifelike illusory objects and decoy duplicates of the caster. |

> **Note for combat design:** a whole-faction kit built on deception — feint-heavy, damage-light. Perfect
> for "fight smart" player fantasy and for enemies that punish button-mashing.
> The technique name "**Foundation of the White Fox**" from the game proposal **could not be verified**;
> if a named cycling/novice technique is needed, label it INVENTED FOR GAME.

### 3.2 The Empty Palm — Lindon's own creation (pure madra)

- Verified canon: **Lindon invents the Empty Palm himself** during *Unsouled*, while training under
  Yerin's brutal sparring at Heaven's Glory. It is the first technique ever built on his "worthless" pure madra.
- Effect: a palm strike driving pure madra **into the opponent's core**, disrupting their madra entirely —
  for a few seconds they cannot use techniques **or even control their limbs**.
- **Game use:** signature Unsouled-start skill; a "silence + stagger" tool that turns enemy power against
  them. Its dramatic debut kills should mirror canon (see Beat 15).

### 3.3 Path of the Endless Sword (Yerin / the Sword Sage) — sword madra

Practiced only by the **Sword Sage (Sage of the Endless Sword)** and his disciple **Yerin**.

| Technique | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Flowing Sword** | Enforcer | Enforces her blade so it gathers sword aura continuously — **hits harder the longer the fight goes**. |
| **Rippling Sword** | Striker | Launches razor crescents of sword madra through the air (later also from her Goldsign arms). |
| **Endless Sword** | Ruler | Resonates the sword aura on **every bladed edge in range** — all nearby blades erupt with cutting force at once; a chain-reaction battlefield wipe. |
| **Hidden Blade** | Forger | Forges a thin razor blade hanging in the air, invisible edge-on. (unverified book-1 appearance) |

Yerin's other assets: a master-grade **white-bladed sword**; her **Goldsign** (gained on-page: a
blood-red bladed limb over her shoulder); the **Steelborn Iron body** (unverified naming in book 1); and
a sealed **blood-aspect parasite** bound under her belt-scar — the red rope-belt around her waist is a
**living blood-madra entity her master sealed** (book 1 only hints; keep mysterious).

### 3.4 Path of Heaven's Glory (Heaven's Glory School) — light madra

- Harvests light aura from **Samara's Ring**. Madra of burning radiance: searing Striker bolts/beams,
  light-Forged weapons and wings (unverified specifics — book 1 names the Path but few techniques).
- Elder Rahm is a **Forger** of this Path; Elder Whitehall a Jade of this Path.
- **INVENTED FOR GAME** (clearly fictional names, flavor-matched): *Glory's Lance* (Striker beam),
  *Halo Brand* (Enforcer — radiant weapon), *Daybreak Edict* (Ruler — ignites ambient light into a
  blinding burst zone), *Forged Radiance* (Forger — hard-light shields/blades).

### 3.5 Other valley kits

- **Golden Sword School:** sword-madra Enforcers wielding goldsteel blades (canon, no named techniques).
- **Holy Wind School:** wind/light/dream weather-zones and storm Strikers (canon, no named techniques).
- **Li and Kazan clan techniques:** canon-silent — use the INVENTED Paths from §1.3 and label all
  technique names as invented.

## 5. Plot Chronology of *Unsouled* (beat list for branching)

Ordered beats. *Format: what happens — [where | who | stakes].*

1. **The Spiritual Origin test (prologue/backstory).** Child Lindon's hand provokes no reaction from the
   testing madra; the First Elder declares him **Unsouled** — denied a Path, forbidden the clan's sacred
   arts, given the wooden "empty" badge. — [Wei clan hall | child Lindon, First Elder, Seisha, Jaran |
   Lindon's entire social existence is set to "cripple."]
2. **The ancestral orus tree.** Bullying by **Wei Mon Teris** and years of dead ends push Lindon into the
   wilds, where he and **Kelsa** find an ancient orus tree; they win a spirit-fruit from it (guardian
   Remnant encounter — unverified details), and the family divides it — Seisha's drudge-knife slicing it
   into shares, Kelsa insisting her "crippled" brother gets his. — [Wilds + Shi household | Lindon, Kelsa,
   Teris, Seisha | Lindon's first real fuel for advancement; family loyalty established.]
3. **Festival preparation.** The **Seven-Year Festival** approaches (hosted in Wei territory this cycle);
   Lindon schemes his way onto the rolls and prepares tricks: scripts, traps, and a sealed **jar of
   Remnant hornets**. — [Wei territory | Lindon, family, clan officials | An Unsouled competing at all is a
   scandal; victory means resources and a future.]
4. **Festival matches & the halfsilver trick.** Lindon wins bout(s) through preparation and rules-lawyering
   rather than power — including exploiting **halfsilver** to foul an opponent's madra (the exact
   halfsilver delivery — gifted bracelet vs. hidden token — is **unverified**; the hornet jar is verified for
   Beat 5). Heaven's Glory's delegation takes offense at an Unsouled winning. — [Festival arena | Lindon,
   Copper-rank opponents, watching elders | Public proof that technique-less cunning beats spirit.]
5. **The exhibition execution.** To appease Heaven's Glory, Patriarch **Wei Jin Sairus** forces Lindon into
   an exhibition match against his grandson **Wei Jin Amon**, two full stages above Lindon. Lindon
   releases the hornet Remnants, disarms Amon, beats him with his own spear, and shoves him out of
   the ring — a win that humiliates everyone it was supposed to soothe. — [Festival arena | Lindon,
   Amon, Sairus, Heaven's Glory observers | Win and be hated, or lose and be erased; either way the clan
   plans to quietly punish him.]
6. **Li Markuth descends; Lindon dies; Suriel intervenes.** The ascended Li clan Grand Patriarch
   **Li Markuth** tears into the Festival from the heavens demanding dominion; Lindon chooses to stand
   with his clan and is killed. **Suriel**, Sixth Judge of the Abidan, arrives: she reverses Markuth's
   slaughter (restoring the dead, Lindon included), ships Markuth off to cosmic trial, and — moved by
   Lindon's doomed courage — breaks protocol to give him: (a) a **vision of the future** in which a
   **Dreadgod, the Wandering Titan, crushes Sacred Valley underfoot** decades hence, killing everyone
   he loves; (b) glimpses of the world's great sacred artists (the power ceiling is *real*); (c) a **warm glass
   marble** she can sense from across the worlds; and (d) directions: go to **Mount Samara** and find
   **Yerin, disciple of the Sword Sage**, who can get him out of the valley. — [Festival grounds /
   outside time | Lindon, Li Markuth, Suriel, the whole valley | The premise of the entire series: get strong
   enough, fast enough, to save the valley — and no one will believe why.]
7. **Elder Whisper's counsel.** Lindon seeks out the white fox in his tower; Whisper — who half-sees the
   coming disaster — confirms the world outside is bigger than the valley's lies and urges Lindon to walk
   his own Path. (Exact placement before the climb is unverified.) — [Whisper's tower, Wei territory |
   Lindon, Elder Whisper | The only elder's blessing he'll ever get; exposition anchor.]
8. **Leaving home / the climb.** Lindon leaves his family (cover stories, a mother's equipment, a father's
   grudging gift — unverified specifics) and climbs **Mount Samara** to present himself to the
   **Heaven's Glory School**, hiding his Unsouled badge. — [Mount Samara slopes | Lindon | If they learn
   what he is, expulsion is the *kind* outcome.]
9. **The Trial of Glorious Ascension.** **Elder Whitehall** herds new disciples up the trial: a stair through
   clouds of light hiding **dangerous Remnants**; a candidate named **Shet dies on the steps**. Lindon
   survives on wits and is admitted. — [Mount Samara trial stairs | Lindon, Whitehall, candidates |
   Entrance exam where failure is literally fatal. **Correction to game proposal:** this is the school
   entrance trial, *not* the festival/tournament name — the tournament is the **Seven-Year Festival**.]
10. **Inside Heaven's Glory.** Disciple chores, elixir stipends, the **Lesser Treasure Hall** under
    **Elder Rahm**; Lindon learns the school **murdered the Sword Sage** for his possessions and keeps
    his disciple **Yerin** under polite siege — an "honored guest" they intend to strip of her master's
    treasures. — [Heaven's Glory compound | Lindon, Rahm, school disciples | Lindon realizes his guides
    out of the valley are the school's enemies.]
11. **Lindon and Yerin's oath.** Lindon slips away to Yerin; after she tests him (and fights off Heaven's
    Glory pressure), they **swear an oath on their souls**: he helps her escape and avenge/recover her
    master's effects; she gets him out of Sacred Valley and teaches him. — [Yerin's guarded house, Mount
    Samara | Lindon, Yerin | Soul-oaths are binding; both bet their futures on a stranger.]
12. **Training montage & the Empty Palm.** Yerin's merciless sparring + parasite-grade cycling drills;
    Lindon invents the **Empty Palm** and finally lands it on her, proving pure madra is a weapon. His
    core grows (orus fruit share, school elixirs — Copper draws near). — [Mount Samara | Lindon, Yerin |
    From mascot to combatant.]
13. **The Sword Sage's Remnant.** The dead Sage's chrome-bladed **Remnant** breaks loose/must be
    faced; **Yerin absorbs her master's Remnant and advances to Gold**, gaining her Goldsign (a bladed
    crimson limb over her shoulder). Mid-book fight vs. **Whitehall and his Irons** around these events:

## 6. Remnants and Dreadbeasts

### 6.1 Remnants

- When a sacred artist (or sacred beast) dies, their spirit tears free as a **Remnant**: a living
  madra-creature in their Path's aspects — glassy, abstract, often nightmarish (the Sword Sage's is a
  thing of chrome limbs and blades).
- Remnants keep **instincts and fragments of skill/memory**, rarely true personality at valley power
  levels; powerful masters leave powerful, cunning Remnants.
- Uses: **advancement to Gold** (absorb one into your core — ideally your own master's, on your own
  Path); **Soulsmithing stock** (see below); captive labor in scripted boundaries (the Wei keep Remnants
  for light/dream harvesting — unverified specifics; Lindon's hornet jar is the book-1 example of sealed
  Remnants as a tool).
- Behavior for AI design: territorial, drawn to madra sources, fight with the dead artist's techniques in
  degraded form; bound by scripts/boundaries; goldsteel repels or harms them (unverified).

### 6.2 Soulsmithing (book-1 level)

- A **Soulsmith** dismantles Remnants into parts — especially **bindings**, the organ-like structures that
  execute a dead artist's techniques — and rebuilds them into **constructs** (functional madra machines:
  lights, tools, weapons, the Thousand-Mile Cloud, Seisha's **drudge** assistant).
- A **drudge** is a Soulsmith's diagnostic construct: it measures madra output, reads aspects, and helps
  disassemble Remnants with minimal loss.
- **Scales** of Forged madra are both the fuel-stock and the currency of this economy.
- Seisha is the game's tutorial Soulsmith; Lindon's fascination with the craft is canon and should flavor
  his item-hoarding mechanics. (Full Soulsmithing rules arrive in book 2 — keep crafting shallow in this game.)

### 6.3 Dreadbeasts

- **Corrupted beasts whose spirits are mangled into their flesh** (by hunger aura / Dreadgod influence —
  later-book canon for the cause). Madra-warped anatomy, mindless aggression.
- **They leave no Remnant** when killed — the spirit is fused with the meat. (Loot: madra-tainted body
  parts only; design consequence: dreadbeasts are XP-poor, danger-rich.)
- Sacred Valley's wilds and the slopes hold dreadbeasts and wild Remnants enough to justify combat
  encounters everywhere outside settlements (book-1 presence is light — most on-page monsters are
  Remnants; dreadbeast-heavy zones are an acceptable INVENTED FOR GAME expansion flagged here).
