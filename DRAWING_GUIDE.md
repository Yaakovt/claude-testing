# LEGENDS OF NORVENNA — Drawing Commission Sheet (all 131 fakemon)

## How to draw them (read this first!)
- **Size:** draw BIG — at least **400×400 pixels** per creature if digital, or fill
  a quarter-page if on paper (then photograph it straight-on, in good light, no shadows).
- **Lines:** clean, dark, CLOSED outlines matter most. Interior detail lines
  (belly, markings, face) simple and confident. No shading needed — I add that.
- **Color:** optional! I will color from the game palette. If a color matters to
  you, scribble a note or small swatch next to the drawing.
- **Pose:** front-facing, turned slightly to the LEFT (battle pose). Dynamic beats
  stiff: mid-step, roaring, floating, lunging. If you feel like it, a BACK view
  (creature looking up and to the right) helps too — otherwise I'll derive it.
- **Label** every drawing with the fakemon's name.
- **Send in batches** of 5–10 pictures right into the chat. I'll pixel-artify each
  at 64×64 in the Gen-3 style, color it, and wire it into the game.
- Evolution lines should feel like the SAME creature growing: keep the signature
  features (listed per line below) across all stages.
